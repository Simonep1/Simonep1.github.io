Model and quantities

Each attacker is simulated independently over n systems. At every step, the penetration score Xi is drawn independently from the same distribution. The Bernoulli model uses 1 for a successful penetration and 0 otherwise, with probability p of success. The Rademacher model assigns +1 or −1 with equal probability, giving a symmetric zero-mean score.
